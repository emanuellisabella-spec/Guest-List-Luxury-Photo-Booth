Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Guest List Luxury Photo Booth ("Guest List," "we," "us") is a sole proprietorship based in Vinings, Georgia, providing digital photo booth rentals for events in the Atlanta area. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, booking an event, or joining our email list, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Contact and booking details. Your name, email address, phone number, event date, event location, and any notes you share when you book or fill out a form.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by third-party payment processors. We do not see or store your full card number. We also keep records of your booking, acceptance of our Service Agreement, and payments.
  • Event photos and videos. Photos, GIFs, and boomerangs captured by the booth at your event, including images of you and your guests, and any contact details guests enter at the booth to receive their photos.
  • Website data. Basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar tools.
  • Communications. Messages you send us by email, text, or form.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and deliver your booking.
  • To take payments and send receipts, reminders, and event details.
  • To deliver photos and digital keepsakes to you and your guests.
  • To answer questions and provide support.
  • To send emails about our services, offers, and updates, only if you opted in.
  • To improve our website and services, and to meet legal obligations.

Email, phone, and text communications

By booking, you agree that we may contact you about your event by email, phone call, and text message. When you check the consent box on our forms, you also agree to receive marketing emails from us. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time by clicking it or by emailing us. Even if you opt out of marketing, we may still send messages about an active booking, such as confirmations, payment receipts, and event logistics.

Event photos and guest images

Guests choose to step up to the booth. Photos taken at your event are delivered to the guest and made available to the event host in an online gallery. Under our Service Agreement, the event host gives us permission to use event photos, videos, and testimonials for marketing, including our website, social media, and advertising, and confirms that any needed parental or guardian consent has been obtained for minors. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, email us and we will review your request.

Guest contact information

Contact details guests enter at the booth are used to deliver their photos. If the booked package includes guest lead capture, those details are also provided to the event host, who is responsible for any consent required to contact guests for marketing. We do not sell guest contact information.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, for example scheduling software (Cal.com), email and form tools (Zoho), payment processors, website hosting, and photo delivery tools. These providers may only use your information to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies and analytics

Our website and booking tools may use cookies to keep the site working, remember preferences, and understand traffic. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the booking tools work.

How long we keep information

We keep booking and payment records as long as needed for business, tax, and legal purposes. Online galleries stay up for 90 days after your event, after which we may delete them. Email subscribers stay on our list until they unsubscribe.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from our email list, by contacting us below. We will respond within a reasonable time. Some records we must keep by law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website and forms are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children may appear in event photos as guests of an event, and a parent or guardian may ask us to remove any photo.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
